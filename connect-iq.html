Privacy Policy for Levi Beaird Connect IQ Watch Faces

Effective date: November 24, 2026
Developer: Levi Beaird
Contact: levi.beaird2020@gmail.com

This privacy policy applies to the following Garmin Connect IQ watch faces developed by Levi Beaird:

  • Deployment Countdown
  • Deployment Countdown Holiday
  • Zulu Pilot
  • Pilot House
  • Greenwich
  • Wyrmsigil
  • Trigpoint

Summary

  • I do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information.
  • These watch faces have no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.
  • Most faces use sensor and activity data only on your watch to draw the display, and they send nothing off the device.
  • Zulu Pilot is the only face that uses the internet. It requests aviation weather (METAR) from NOAA Aviation Weather through your paired phone.

Information used on your watch

The watch faces may read information already available on your Garmin device so they can display it, such as:

  • Time and date
  • Heart rate
  • Steps and other activity data
  • Battery level
  • Barometer / pressure and altitude
  • Notification count
  • Last-known position, used only on the watch. For example, Trigpoint uses it for grid coordinates and sun times. Pilot House and Greenwich may use it for sun times and related on-watch calculations.

This information stays on your watch. I do not receive it, store it, or have access to it.

Zulu Pilot and weather data

Zulu Pilot can download METAR weather from NOAA Aviation Weather through your paired phone’s internet connection.

  • When you set a favorite airport, the watch face sends that airport’s ICAO code in order to request its weather report.
  • In nearest-airport mode, the watch face uses your watch’s last-known position (coordinates) to request nearby weather.
  • Weather requests go to NOAA Aviation Weather. I do not operate that service, and I do not receive, store, or log those requests.
  • Nothing is sold or shared by me.

METAR updates require your phone to be connected to the internet. If the phone is not connected, the face continues to show previously received or on-watch information.

What I do not do

  • I do not create accounts or require you to sign in.
  • I do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking.
  • I do not sell, rent, or share personal information.
  • I do not store personal information on my own servers. These watch faces have no developer-operated backend.

Garmin Connect and Connect IQ

These watch faces run on Garmin devices through Connect IQ. Garmin may process information in connection with Garmin Connect, Connect IQ, device sync, and the Connect IQ Store under Garmin’s own privacy policy. I do not control Garmin’s practices. Please see Garmin’s privacy policy.

Data retention

Because I do not collect or store your information, there is no developer-held data to retain or delete. You can stop any on-watch use of data by changing the watch face settings or removing the watch face from your device.

Children’s privacy

These watch faces are not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
