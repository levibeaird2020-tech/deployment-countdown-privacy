# Privacy Policy – Deployment Countdown

**Effective date:** October 20, 2026
**App:** Deployment Countdown (Android, package `com.beaird.deployment_countdown`)
**Developer:** Levi Beaird (individual developer)
**Contact:** levi.beaird2020@gmail.com

Deployment Countdown is a personal app that counts down the days of a
military deployment and lets the deployed member and their family keep text,
voice, and video messages in one place. This policy explains what the app does
with your information.

> Deployment Countdown is an independent app. It is **not** an official app of,
> and is not affiliated with or endorsed by, the U.S. Department of Defense,
> the Department of Homeland Security, or any military branch.

## 1. Summary
* **The current version keeps everything on your phone.** It does not send
  your information to the developer or to anyone else.
* No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data.
* You can delete everything at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing
  its storage.

## 2. Information the app stores (on your device only)
| What | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Your first name / display name and role (deployed member or family/friend) | Shown on the countdown and on your messages | App storage on your phone |
| Deployment details: the deployed person's name, branch theme, start and return dates, invite code | To calculate and show the countdown | App storage on your phone |
| Text messages you write | Shown in the Messages screen | App storage on your phone |
| Voice clips you record (microphone) | Played back in Messages | App's private files folder on your phone |
| Video clips you record (camera) or pick from your gallery | Played back in Messages | App's private files folder on your phone |
| A simple app-open counter | To change the first background image each time you open the app | App storage on your phone |

The app only uses the **camera** and **microphone** when you tap the record
buttons, and Android asks for your permission first. You can turn these
permissions off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Deployment Countdown
→ Permissions. The app does not access your contacts, location, or files
other than a video you choose.

## 3. What we do not do
* We do not collect, upload, or share your information in the current version.
* We do not use advertising, advertising IDs, or analytics/crash-reporting services.
* We do not sell personal information.

## 4. Planned: sharing with your family through Firebase
A future update will let family members on other phones join your countdown
with an invite code. When that feature is turned on (the app's Settings
screen will no longer say "Demo mode"), the app will use **Google Firebase**
(Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage), operated by
Google LLC, to:
* create an anonymous account ID for your device (no email or phone needed);
* store your display name, role, countdown details, invite code, and the
  messages, voice clips, and videos you send, so the members of your group can
  see them;
* encrypt data in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Google also encrypts it at rest.

Only people who join your group with your invite code can read that group's
messages. The developer does not use this data for any other purpose. Google
processes it on our behalf under the Firebase terms
(https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). **This policy will be updated
before that feature is released**, and the update will say how long data is
kept and how to delete it from the server (deleting your group or messages in
the app, or emailing the contact address above).

## 5. Data retention and deletion
In the current version, data stays until you delete it: leave/reset the
countdown in the app's Settings, clear the app's storage
(Settings → Apps → Deployment Countdown → Storage → Clear storage), or
uninstall the app.

## 6. Security
Data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a
normal (non-rooted) Android device. Use a screen lock to protect your phone.

## 7. Children
The app is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect information from children. A parent may let a
child view the countdown on the parent's phone.

## 8. Operational security reminder
Please don't put classified or sensitive operational details (locations, ship
or unit movements, exact dates) in messages. Follow your command's OPSEC and
social-media guidance.

## 9. Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a
new effective date. Important changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
